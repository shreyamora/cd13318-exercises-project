NASA Mission Intelligence RAG - Evaluation Dataset
===================================================

This file contains the sample questions used to test the NASA RAG chat
application end-to-end, along with the responses the system is expected to
produce. Each question was asked through the Streamlit chat interface
(`streamlit run chat.py`) after the embedding pipeline had processed the
documents in the `data_text/` folder. RAGAS faithfulness and answer-relevancy
scores were observed in the sidebar for each answer.

---------------------------------------------------
Q1. What was the objective of the Apollo 11 mission?
---------------------------------------------------
Expected response:
Apollo 11 was the first crewed mission to land humans on the Moon. Its primary
objective was to perform a crewed lunar landing and return safely to Earth,
fulfilling the national goal of landing astronauts on the Moon before the end
of the 1960s. Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin landed the Lunar Module while
Michael Collins remained in lunar orbit aboard the Command Module.
Source: apollo_11 mission transcripts / flight plan.

Expected quality: high faithfulness (answer grounded in retrieved Apollo 11
context), high answer relevancy.

-------------------------------------------------------------
Q2. Who were the crew members of Apollo 11?
-------------------------------------------------------------
Expected response:
The Apollo 11 crew consisted of Commander Neil A. Armstrong, Command Module
Pilot Michael Collins, and Lunar Module Pilot Edwin "Buzz" Aldrin.
Source: apollo_11 command module / PAO transcripts.

Expected quality: high faithfulness and relevancy.

-------------------------------------------------------------
Q3. What went wrong during the Apollo 13 mission?
-------------------------------------------------------------
Expected response:
During Apollo 13, an oxygen tank in the Service Module ruptured/exploded roughly
two days into the flight, crippling the spacecraft's normal power, oxygen, and
water supplies. The Moon landing was aborted, and the crew used the Lunar Module
as a "lifeboat" to survive and return safely to Earth. This is the origin of the
famous phrase "Houston, we've had a problem."
Source: apollo_13 technical / command module transcripts.

Expected quality: high faithfulness when Apollo 13 documents are retrieved.

-------------------------------------------------------------
Q4. How did the Apollo 13 crew return safely to Earth?
-------------------------------------------------------------
Expected response:
After the oxygen tank failure, mission control and the crew powered down the
Command Module to conserve resources and used the Lunar Module Aquarius for
propulsion, oxygen, and power. They performed a free-return trajectory around the
Moon and improvised a carbon-dioxide scrubber adapter before re-entering with the
Command Module for a safe splashdown.
Source: apollo_13 technical / PAO transcripts.

Expected quality: high relevancy; faithfulness depends on retrieved detail.

-------------------------------------------------------------
Q5. What happened during the Challenger STS-51L mission?
-------------------------------------------------------------
Expected response:
The Space Shuttle Challenger mission STS-51L ended in tragedy when the orbiter
broke apart approximately 73 seconds after launch on January 28, 1986, resulting
in the loss of all seven crew members. The mission audio transcripts capture the
communications around the launch.
Source: challenger STS-51L mission audio transcripts.

Expected quality: high faithfulness when Challenger audio transcripts are
retrieved.

-------------------------------------------------------------
Q6. (Filtering test) Using the Apollo 13 mission filter, what
	was the flight plan focus?
-------------------------------------------------------------
Expected response:
With the mission filter set to apollo_13, the retrieved context should come only
from Apollo 13 documents. The answer should describe Apollo 13 procedures and
mission operations without pulling in Apollo 11 or Challenger content, verifying
that metadata filtering works correctly.

Expected quality: retrieved contexts all belong to apollo_13 (filter working).

-------------------------------------------------------------
Q7. (Edge case) What is the capital of France?
-------------------------------------------------------------
Expected response:
This is an out-of-scope question with no supporting NASA context. The assistant
should indicate that the provided mission documents do not contain information to
answer the question, rather than fabricating an answer.

Expected quality: low relevancy to retrieved NASA context; validates that the
system does not hallucinate beyond its documents.
